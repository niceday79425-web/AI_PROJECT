Risk Factors

Investing in pharmaceutical giants like Merck (MRK) comes with inherent risks, despite its established market position.

  1. Patent Expirations & Generic Competition: The most significant long-term risk for Merck is the eventual patent expiration of its blockbuster drug, Keytruda. While the company is actively developing new therapies and extending patent life where possible, the loss of exclusivity for such a major revenue driver could lead to substantial revenue declines from generic competition.
  2. R&D Pipeline & Clinical Trial Failures: Merck's future growth heavily relies on its research and development pipeline. The failure of key clinical trials, delays in regulatory approvals, or unexpected side effects during development can lead to significant financial setbacks and impact investor confidence.
  3. Regulatory & Pricing Pressures: The pharmaceutical industry faces increasing scrutiny over drug pricing globally, particularly in the US. Potential legislative changes, increased government intervention, and public pressure to reduce drug costs could negatively impact Merck's profitability and revenue growth.

Conclusion & Investor Action Points

While challenges like patent expirations and R&D risks persist, Merck's strategic investments in new therapeutic areas and robust financial health position it well for future growth.
