Recent Performance & Key Events

TSLA's jump to $367.95 marks an impressive short-term rebound, outperforming the broader S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite benchmarks. Key catalysts influencing recent sentiment include:

  • Autonomous Driving & Full Self-Driving (FSD) Progress: Heightened investor focus on regulatory approvals and software monetization schedules across North America and international markets.

Risk Factors

  • Regulatory & Legal Headwinds: Autonomous driving regulatory scrutiny and potential delays in commercial Robotaxi deployment timelines.
