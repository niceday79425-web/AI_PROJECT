The recent 6.90% drop in GEV's share price to $1004.53 reflects broader macroeconomic pressures rather than isolated company failures. Risk Factors

Investing in GEV at this valuation carries several inherent risks that must be carefully managed:

  • Macroeconomic Sensitivity: As a capital-intensive business, GEV is highly sensitive to rising interest rates and bond yields, which increase borrowing costs and can delay massive infrastructure projects.
  • Supply Chain Bottlenecks: The global energy transition relies on complex, cross-border supply chains for rare minerals and specialized components. Any escalation in geopolitical tensions could disrupt production lines.
  • Execution Risks in Offshore Wind: While onshore power and grid electrification have shown high profitability, the offshore wind division continues to face regulatory, logistical, and operational hurdles that could impact near-term margins.
