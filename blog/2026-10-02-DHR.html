  • Regulatory & Compliance Risks: Operating in highly regulated sectors (healthcare, environmental), DHR faces ongoing risks related to changing regulations, compliance costs, and potential for product recalls or legal challenges.
  • Intense Competition & Innovation Pace: The life sciences and diagnostics markets are highly competitive and driven by rapid technological advancements. DHR must continually innovate and acquire new technologies to maintain its competitive edge against well-capitalized rivals.
