Dividend Investor Perspective

While Ethereum is not an equity that distributes statutory corporate dividends, income-focused investors often view Ethereum staking yields as a digital counterpart to traditional dividends. Currently, consensus staking APR hovers around 3.1% to 3.4%, paid natively in ETH.

Unlike traditional equities with corporate payout ratios, staking rewards are algorithmically governed by protocol inflation and network fee burns (EIP-1559). While spot ETFs in the US do not yet distribute staking yield due to regulatory parameters, direct holders gain compounding income.
